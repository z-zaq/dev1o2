Ledger №6 — About

Built to be checked,
not just trusted.

Avalon Capital Miner started from a simple idea: an account should show its work. Every balance you see is the sum of a visible transaction history, not a number we tell you to trust.

How we operate

Three principles, kept short on purpose.

01

Transparent by default

Every deposit, withdrawal, and transfer is logged and visible to you from your dashboard the moment it happens.

02

Plain language, no jargon

We explain what an account action does before you take it — not after, and not in fine print.

03

Support from people

Questions about your account are answered by our team, not routed through an automated script.
